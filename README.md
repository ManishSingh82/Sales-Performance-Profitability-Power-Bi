# Sales-Performance-Profitability-Power-Bi


## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Sales Performance & Profitability Dashboard** built using **Power BI**.  
The dashboard analyzes **revenue, profit, discounts, shipping costs, customer segments, and regional performance** to identify **profit leakage** and support data-driven business decisions.

Despite strong revenue generation, the business experiences **negative profitability**, making it critical to understand why sales are not converting into profit.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
The company faces the following challenges:
- High revenue but **negative overall profit**
- Excessive **discounting** without understanding its impact
- High **shipping costs** reducing margins
- Limited visibility into **loss-making products, regions, and customer segments**

**Objective:**  
To build a single dashboard that helps stakeholders identify **where and why profits are being lost**.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Power BI  
- DAX  
- Excel / CSV Dataset  
- Data Modeling & Visualization  

---

## 📂 Dataset Description
The dataset includes:
- Order details (Order ID, Date)
- Product information (Category, Sub-category, Product Name)
- Customer segments
- Geographic data (Region, State)
- Sales, Profit, Discount, Shipping Cost

## 🧠 Key Business Insights
- High discounts negatively impact profit margins  
- Strong sales alone do not guarantee profitability  
- Shipping costs are a major contributor to losses  
- Certain products and regions require immediate optimization  

---

## 📌 Business Recommendations
- Introduce controlled discount thresholds  
- Optimize logistics and shipping strategies  
- Reprice or discontinue consistently loss-making products  
- Focus on profitable customer segments and regions
